AMBEDKAR MEMORIAL — POLISHED LIFE UI

Hardware:
Arduino UNO R4 WiFi + MAR3501 / ILI9486, 480x320 landscape.

Touch:
XP=D8, XM=D9, YP=A3, YM=A2
Soft-touch threshold: TS_MINP=80
Verified calibration unchanged.

LIFE now has five fixed story cards:
1. The Beginning — 14 April 1891, Mhow
2. Education — 1913 to 1916, Columbia and London
3. Social Reform — Mahad, 1927
4. Constitution — 1947
5. Final Chapter — 1956

Design constraints:
- Body text is kept large.
- No vertical scrolling.
- No long paragraph is forced into one screen.
- Image and text occupy separate regions.
- Header, date, body and navigation have fixed safe areas.
- Swipe left/right changes cards; no continuous redraw animation.

Source basis:
PIB and Dr Ambedkar Foundation / Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment material.
